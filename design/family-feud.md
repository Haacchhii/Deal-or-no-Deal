# Family Feud addition

An extension of the existing Larong Pinoy game hub. Host-led play was selected by the user. Preserve the existing Deal or No Deal and Pinoy Henyo flows.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A readable shared answer board operated by one host, with deliberate answer reveals and reversible scoring.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the current cream, ink, gold, editorial serif headings, and rectangular controls. Family Feud uses the existing forest-green token to distinguish its game tile and board.

STORY: Choose the third game, edit team names and question blocks privately, conduct the face-off aloud, reveal answers, track strikes, resolve a steal, and compare final scores.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Team totals above the question and numbered answer board. Round bank and multiplier sit beside the round count; host actions and strikes sit below the board. On phones, answers become one column.

FORM: Local extension of the established hub; no new visual-world seed or generated artwork. Custom content is escaped before rendering. Fictional practice points are labeled explicitly.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

The existing design is preserved; no new global DESIGN.md or shipping raster is required for this extension.
