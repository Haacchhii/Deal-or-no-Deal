# Condo gallery — first visual design

Status: desktop visual concepts, not a published or interactive website.

## Purpose

Help office staff and prospective tenants browse unit photographs before a personal viewing. The first building contains approximately 55–60 units across Towers A and B. The owner supplies photographs; this showcase does not assert availability.

## Direction

A boutique residence aesthetic: deep forest green (#183C34), crisp ivory (#F7F7F2), charcoal text, large editorial serif headings and simple sans-serif navigation. The visual emphasis stays on the photography. Minimal borders, open layouts and generous spacing provide the finesse without making the unit directory complicated.

The working name “The Residences,” its symbol, and all generated interiors are placeholders. They are not a proposed final business identity or photographs of actual units. Unit numbers other than the supplied 2103B and 1504A are illustrative examples, not a verified inventory.

## Three screens

1. Landing page: large interior photograph, concise introduction and direct link to the units. Tower links provide a shorter route for repeat office use.
2. Directory: Tower A / Tower B navigation, units grouped by floor, and a photograph plus real unit number for each album. No property detail cards or availability controls.
3. Album: unit identifier, tower and floor, a dominant image, previous/next controls, thumbnails and full-screen viewing.

## Unit numbering

- 2103B → Tower B, floor 21, unit 03.
- 1504A → Tower A, floor 15, unit 04.
- Preserve the complete identifier in headings and links, including the leading zero within the unit component.
- Confirm any additional numbering patterns against the supplied inventory before automatic parsing.

## Implementation intent

Keep tower selection and returning to the directory immediate. Preserve the selected tower and floor when returning from an album. On mobile, stack directory imagery and keep touch controls generous. Provide visible keyboard focus, keyboard gallery navigation and Escape to exit the full-screen viewer. Support reduced motion; use only restrained image transitions and subtle link feedback.

Optimize display copies of the supplied originals. Load directory thumbnails first and larger photographs on demand. Do not create 60 fictional albums to imply a complete inventory.

## Current boundary

Photo display only. Prices, availability, room specifications, advanced filters, bookings, payments, accounts and additional locations remain outside this first design.

## Needed for real content

Business name/logo, approved photographs grouped by unit number, and the actual unit list. The visual layouts can be reviewed before those arrive.
