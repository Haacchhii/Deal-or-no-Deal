## Relationship to the original catalog

Commit `5cb41da` established the employee-supplied baseline with 53 catalog entries. Two entries represented combined units (`1619A-1620A` and `1224-25B`), so entry counts and individual door counts are not interchangeable.

Compared with that baseline:

- `1912B` and `1836A` are confirmed additions.
- `1214A` was added during an intermediate catalog update but is absent from the client's confirmed inventory, so it was removed from the public catalog.
- `1213A` appeared in the supplied photo folders but is absent from the confirmed inventory and remains excluded.
- Photos originally supplied under `1023A (Room)` are represented as the Bedroom album for confirmed unit `1023B`; `1023A` is not a separate catalog unit.
